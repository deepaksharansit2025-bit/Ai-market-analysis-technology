01 / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A new layer of digital work

Generative artificial intelligence (Generative AI) is a class of AI that creates new content—such as text, images, audio, video, and software code—based on patterns learned from data. It includes large language models and other generative models.

Businesses are exploring it to speed up routine work, improve customer support, support software development, personalize services, and help employees work with information. Market potential is significant, but value depends on reliable outputs, secure data practices, effective integration, and measurable business results.

Research note: This report is a qualitative market analysis, not a financial forecast. Market size and adoption vary by definition, geography, and research provider. See the linked sources for current published estimates.

04 / STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

SWOT analysis

A balanced view of the technology's commercial potential and limitations.

S

Strengths

  • Rapid drafting and summarization
  • Flexible support across many tasks
  • Can be integrated into existing workflows
W

Weaknesses

  • Can produce incorrect or invented answers
  • Output quality varies by task
  • Computing and review costs can add up
O

Opportunities

  • Industry-specific assistants
  • Multilingual and accessible services
  • New AI-enabled products and workflows
T

Threats

  • Privacy, security, and copyright concerns
  • Changing regulations and standards
  • Competition and rapid model changes

05 / RISKS & RESPONSE

Adoption requires responsible design

RiskRecommended response
Incorrect output

Verify important claims, cite trusted sources, and keep human review for high-impact decisions.

Data privacy

Do not submit confidential data to unapproved tools; apply access controls and retention rules.

Bias and fairness

Test outputs with diverse cases and provide a way to report and correct problems.

Copyright and ownership

Review licensing, permissions, and organizational policies before publishing generated material.

Unclear return on investment

Run a small pilot with baseline measures, user feedback, and clear success criteria.

06 / FUTURE OUTLOOK

From experimentation to measurable impact

Over the next several years, market development is likely to depend less on novelty alone and more on whether organizations can deploy useful, secure, and dependable systems at a sustainable cost. Smaller specialized models, multimodal tools, AI agents, and deeper workflow integration are areas to monitor.

Adoption will not be uniform. Highly regulated or safety-critical sectors may move more cautiously, while teams with clear use cases and strong data governance may progress faster.

07 / CONCLUSION

The opportunity is real—but execution matters.

Generative AI is an important emerging technology with applications across many industries. Its market opportunity is supported by demand for productivity, easier access to AI tools, and integration into digital products. However, successful adoption requires realistic expectations, data protection, human oversight, and evidence of business value. Organizations should begin with a clearly defined problem, evaluate outcomes, and scale only when the results justify it.
